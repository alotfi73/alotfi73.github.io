Data/Image digitizer

Measure areas and read data from chart images

Calibrate the axes, trace an area or pick it by colour, and get real values on linear or log scales. Everything runs in your browser, so the image never leaves your computer.

Alireza Lotfollahzade, EITUpdated 7 November 2026Exact polygon integration, pixel-weighted colour regions

Method

How the numbers are computed

Four reference points fix the mapping from pixels to axis values. The line through X1 and X2 is a line of constant Y, and the line through Y1 and Y2 is a line of constant X. That gives an affine map, so rotated or slightly skewed scans are handled without extra steps.

u = a·xpx + b·ypx + e v = c·xpx + d·ypx + f(1)

Here u = X on a linear axis and u = log10 X on a log axis; v is the same for Y. A shape drawn with straight edges is a polygon in (u, v), and its real area follows from Green’s theorem, integrated exactly along every edge:

A = | ∮ X dY | = | Σedges ∫ X(u) · Y′(v) dv |(2)

Colour regions are measured pixel by pixel. Each pixel adds the real area it covers, which grows with X and Y on log axes:

A = Σpixels |ad − bc| · (ln 10 · X) · (ln 10 · Y)(3)

The ln 10 · X factor applies only to a log X axis, and ln 10 · Y only to a log Y axis. A ratio is the sub area divided by the main area in real units. The tool also reports the on-screen (pixel) ratio and how much of the sub area lies inside the main area.

Why log areas look wrong. On a log–log plot, a square one decade on each side holds 81 units² between 1 and 10, but 8 100 units² between 10 and 100. The tool reports the real area and, next to it, the axis-space area in decades².

Accuracy. Pick reference points far apart and zoom in before clicking. A 1 px error over a 500 px span is 0.2 % of the axis.

Formats. Browsers open PNG, JPG, GIF, BMP, WebP and SVG. Export PDF or TIFF figures to PNG first.

Notes

What to watch for

  • JPEG compression blurs colours. A colour tolerance of 30–60 usually separates a filled region cleanly; tick “Fill holes” when grid lines or labels cut through it.
  • A freehand shape that crosses itself is measured with signed areas. Draw around the region without crossing.
  • “Save project” stores the image, calibration, shapes and points in one file, so a measurement can be checked or repeated later.
