About

Engineering knowledge, with the working shown.

This site shares chemical process engineering with students and practising engineers: lessons on equipment and methods, calculators and tools that run in your browser, and practice examples with their solutions. Every page names its assumptions, labels illustrative numbers and lists its sources.
